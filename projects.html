Chess Coach

A local tool that analyzes your Chess.com games for practical mistakes — moves players near your rating would realistically find — instead of just Stockfish's objectively-best line. Runs Stockfish alongside Maia, a neural net trained on human games at specific rating bands, to separate practical misses from common mistakes at your level and engine-only finds nobody would spot over the board.
