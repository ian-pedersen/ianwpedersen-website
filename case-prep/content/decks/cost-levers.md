## Q: Brainstorm: list typical variable costs.
@tags cost, brainstorm
- Raw materials and manufacturing supplies
- Direct labor
- Sales commissions and bonuses
- Shipping and packaging
- Distribution commissions (e.g. booking platforms)
- Credit card transaction fees
- Outsourced services
- Discounts
- Import and export duties

## Q: Brainstorm: list typical fixed costs.
@tags cost, brainstorm
- Rent and leases (incl. airport fees)
- Utilities
- Corporate salaries
- Maintenance
- Marketing
- Overhead (central services, management)
- Depreciation
- R&D

## Q: Brainstorm: what are the four buckets of ways to decrease costs?
@tags cost, brainstorm
1. **Labor**
2. **Production / operations**
3. **External stakeholders** (suppliers, facilities)
4. **Other expenses** (marketing, R&D, overhead)

## Q: Brainstorm: ways to cut labor costs.
@tags cost, brainstorm
- Cut overtime
- Reduce benefits or salaries
- Cross-train workers
- Optimize roles, reduce indirect staff
- Outsource
- Layoffs (last resort)

## Q: Brainstorm: ways to cut production and operations costs.
@tags cost, brainstorm
- Improve internal processes
- Invest in new technology
- Consolidate production space
- Flexible production lines
- Reduce inventory
- Outsource or import parts
- Relocate to a cheaper region

## Q: Brainstorm: ways to cut costs with suppliers, facilities and other expenses.
@tags cost, brainstorm
- **Suppliers**: renegotiate, find cheaper vendors, consolidate for volume discounts
- **Facilities**: renegotiate leases, downsize (remote work), sublease
- **Other**: lower marketing and CAC, trim R&D, cut unused licenses, reduce overhead (e.g. data storage)

## Q: Why might costs be rising? Structure your brainstorm.
@tags cost, brainstorm, diagnosis
- **Price per unit**: out-of-date contracts, pricier suppliers, small or infrequent orders (no scale), onshore vs. cheaper outsourced, more expensive materials
- **Volume of units**: more waste, no effort to limit unneeded use, customers using more than needed, no standard procedures for staff
- Or by type: **fixed** up (new hires, rent) vs. **variable** up (taxes, supplier issues, tariffs)

## Q: Brainstorm: how can a plant maximize production?
@tags operations, brainstorm
- **Equipment**: fewer and faster maintenance stops, fix root causes of breakdowns, faster changeovers, newer machines
- **Labor productivity**: training, hiring standards, production KPIs, incentives, extra shifts to use machines more
- **Output quality**: better materials, fewer defects, fewer spec mismatches
