## Q: How do you structure "how should we respond to a competitive threat?"
@tags competitive-response, structure
1. **Potential impact on us**: which segments are exposed, how much could we lose?
2. **What the competitor is after**: how big and profitable is the opportunity they're pursuing?
3. **Possible responses**, and which one the impact justifies

## Q: What are the possible responses to a competitive threat?
@tags competitive-response
- **Do nothing**: the threat is small or not aimed at our core
- **Mitigate**: protect what we have (retain key clients, loyalty, targeted price cuts)
- **Align / replicate**: launch a competing offer
- **Collaborate**: partner with, or acquire, the newcomer

## Q: A low-cost online optician is entering the market of a national eyewear chain. How should the chain respond? Structure it.
@mode recall
@tags competitive-response, structure
1. **Impact on us**
   - Which segments overlap (price-sensitive, repeat prescriptions)?
   - Estimated revenue and profit at risk
2. **The entrant's opportunity**
   - Size and profitability of online eyewear
   - Their advantages: cost, convenience; their weak spots: fittings, eye exams
3. **Response options**
   - Do nothing, mitigate (retain loyal customers, bundle exams), match (own online offer), collaborate or acquire
   - Cost, speed and risk of each, plus how they might counter
