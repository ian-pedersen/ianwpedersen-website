## Q: Retail: what is the core store revenue equation?
@tags formula, revenue
**Store revenue = traffic × conversion × AOV.**

## Q: Retail: define conversion rate and AOV.
@tags metric, formula
- **Conversion** = buyers ÷ visitors
- **AOV** = revenue ÷ transactions

## Q: Retail: define sales per sq ft and inventory turnover.
@tags metric, formula
- **Sales per sq ft** = revenue ÷ selling space. Compares stores of different sizes.
- **Inventory turnover** = COGS ÷ average inventory. Higher means leaner.

## Q: What are same-store sales (comps), and why do they matter more than total sales growth?
@tags metric, quiz
Growth from stores open at least a year, excluding new openings. Total growth can be bought by opening stores; comps show whether the **existing** business is healthy.

## Q: Traffic is up 10% but sales are flat. What's happening?
@tags quiz, case-angle
Conversion and/or AOV fell. Check stock-outs, staffing and checkout lines, pricing, assortment, and whether new visitors are browsers (e.g. a promo or event drawing low-intent traffic).

## Q: Why are Costco's membership fees so important to its model?
@tags quiz, revenue
Fees are almost pure profit and a large share of operating income. That lets Costco price merchandise near cost, which drives traffic and renewals: a flywheel.

## Q: Retail: name the business models.
@tags players
- Brick-and-mortar
- Pure-play online (Amazon, ASOS)
- Omnichannel (Target, Nike)
- Discount (Costco, Dollar General): high volume, thin margin
- Luxury (Louis Vuitton): brand and pricing power

## Q: Retail: what are the main cost buckets?
@tags cost
- COGS (sets gross margin)
- Rent and occupancy
- Store labor (turnover adds cost)
- Marketing
- Inventory carrying: warehousing, shrink, markdowns
- E-commerce: fulfillment, shipping, returns

## Q: Retail promotion profitability: what do you weigh?
@tags case-angle
Incremental volume vs. **margin given up**, minus **cannibalization** of full-price sales and **pull-forward** of future purchases.

## Q: Retail: what are the typical case angles?
@tags case-angle
- Promotion profitability
- New store: catchment demographics, competition, sales/sq ft benchmarks, payback
- Cost reduction: labor scheduling, shrink, footprint
- Online vs. store mix, fulfillment economics
