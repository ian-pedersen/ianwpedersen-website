## Q: Brainstorm: what are the five buckets of ways to increase revenue?
@tags revenue, brainstorm
1. **Pricing**
2. **Marketing** (existing and new customers)
3. **Distribution** and operations
4. **Innovation** and inorganic growth
5. **Customers**: current vs. new

## Q: Brainstorm: pricing ideas to increase revenue.
@tags revenue, pricing, brainstorm
- Raise or lower price to the optimal point
- Price tiers: customization, premium versions
- Segment-based pricing
- Bundles and combos, cross-sell
- Promotions and discounts
- Subscription model (two-part tariff)
- Dynamic pricing by location or time
- Cashback (B2C), rebates (B2B)

## Q: Brainstorm: marketing ideas to grow revenue from existing customers.
@tags revenue, marketing, brainstorm
- Customer experience and engagement
- Loyalty programs
- Longer opening hours
- Warranties
- Faster service
- Building a community
- Using customer data to get closer to the customer
- Follow-ups and pop-ups
- Gamification

## Q: Brainstorm: marketing ideas to win new customers.
@tags revenue, marketing, brainstorm
- Advertising
- Free trials
- Referral programs
- Social media and influencers
- Branding
- Events and sponsorships
- Conferences and panels, published articles (B2B)

## Q: Brainstorm: distribution and operations ideas to grow revenue.
@tags revenue, brainstorm
- New channels: e-commerce, mobile app, delivery, physical stores
- Partnerships, direct-to-consumer
- New geographies
- Train distribution and sales staff

## Q: Brainstorm: innovation and inorganic ideas to grow revenue.
@tags revenue, brainstorm
- Current products: new packaging, versions
- New offerings for the same customers (no extra CAC)
- R&D into new businesses and customers
- Inorganic: acquire a competitor or new business
- Joint venture: cheaper, shares resources, risk and scale
- Partnership: cheapest, gives access to brand and customers

## Q: Brainstorm: new customer segments to target.
@tags revenue, brainstorm
Gen Z, college students, B2B, new geographies, new scope (adjacent needs), segments reached through influencers and social media.

## Q: Why might revenue be declining? Structure your brainstorm.
@tags revenue, brainstorm, diagnosis
- **Volume**: less demand, customer churn, no new clients, poor distribution
- **Price**: more competition and price pressure, loss of pricing power
