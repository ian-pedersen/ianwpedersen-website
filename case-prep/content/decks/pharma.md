## Q: Pharma: what are the core economics?
@tags overview
Huge, risky up-front R&D → patent-protected high margins → the **patent cliff** when generics or biosimilars arrive.

## Q: Pharma: who are the players along the value chain?
@tags players
- Discovery and development: big pharma, biotech
- Manufacturers: branded vs. generic
- CROs that run trials (IQVIA)
- Distributors (McKesson) and pharmacies
- **PBMs** (Express Scripts, CVS Caremark): negotiate prices and rebates for payers

## Q: Roughly what does it cost to bring one approved drug to market, and why does that include failed drugs?
@tags cost, quiz
About **$2.6B** (Tufts estimate). Only ~10% of candidates make it from Phase I to approval, so the winners have to pay for all the failures.

## Q: Pharma: what is the Phase I-to-approval success rate?
@tags metric
Roughly **~10%**.

## Q: Pharma: which metrics matter?
@tags metric
- Pipeline depth by phase
- Phase transition / success rates
- Trial timelines and time-to-market (more patent-protected years)
- Sales per rep
- Patient outcomes (for value-based contracts)
- Peak sales and years to LOE (loss of exclusivity)

## Q: A blockbuster loses exclusivity in 3 years. What are three ways to fill the gap?
@tags quiz, case-angle
- **Acquire or license** late-stage assets
- **Lifecycle management**: new indications, formulations, combinations
- Accelerate the internal pipeline
- Launch an authorized generic, or cut costs to protect margin

## Q: Pharma Phase III go/no-go: what's the calculation?
@tags case-angle, formula
**Expected value = P(success) × value of peak-sales economics − trial cost.** For assets, use risk-adjusted NPV (rNPV).

## Q: Pharma market sizing: what's the funnel?
@tags case-angle, formula
Patients with the condition × diagnosis rate × treatment rate × share × price × compliance.

## Q: What role do PBMs play, and why are they controversial?
@tags quiz, players
They manage drug benefits for insurers and employers: build formularies and negotiate rebates from manufacturers. Critics say rebate-based pay rewards higher list prices and the system is opaque.

## Q: Pharma: what are the main cost buckets?
@tags cost
- R&D
- Manufacturing (biologics cost more than small molecules)
- Sales and marketing: reps, DTC ads
- Regulatory compliance (FDA)
- Distribution and rebates through wholesalers, pharmacies and PBMs
