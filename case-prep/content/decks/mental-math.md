## Q: Fractions as percentages: 1/3, 1/6, 1/7, 1/8, 1/9.
@tags mental-math
1/3 = 33.3%, 1/6 = 16.7%, 1/7 ≈ 14.3%, 1/8 = 12.5%, 1/9 ≈ 11.1%

## Q: Fractions as percentages: 1/11, 1/12, 1/15, 1/16, 1/20.
@tags mental-math
1/11 ≈ 9.1%, 1/12 ≈ 8.3%, 1/15 ≈ 6.7%, 1/16 = 6.25%, 1/20 = 5%

## Q: How do you multiply by 25 or 50 quickly?
@tags mental-math
- × 25: multiply by 100, divide by 4
- × 50: multiply by 100, divide by 2

## Q: How do you calculate a percentage of a number quickly (e.g. 15% of 360)?
@tags mental-math
Split it: 10% = 36, 5% = 18 → **54**. Also, x% of y = y% of x (e.g. 8% of 50 = 50% of 8 = 4).

## Q: How do you keep zeros straight with large numbers?
@tags mental-math
Use scientific shorthand: thousand = 10³, million = 10⁶, billion = 10⁹. Multiply the leading digits, add the exponents. E.g. 30M × $40 = 3 × 4 × 10⁷ × 10 = $1.2B.

## Q: How do you estimate growth over several years without a calculator?
@tags mental-math, growth
For small rates, n years at g% ≈ n × g% (slightly more because of compounding). For larger rates or long periods, use the rule of 72.
