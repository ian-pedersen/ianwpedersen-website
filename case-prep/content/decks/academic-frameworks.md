## Q: Supply and demand: when is it useful in a case?
@tags structure, academic
To explain **price changes**, forecast prices and profits, analyze markets outside business (e.g. housing, labor), and diagnose operations (capacity vs. demand). Price and quantity settle where the supply and demand curves cross.

## Q: What are the 3Cs, and how do they map to supply and demand?
@tags structure, academic
- **Company** and **Competitors**: the supply side
- **Customers**: the demand side

## Q: What are Porter's five forces, and what are they for?
@tags structure, academic
Judging how **attractive an industry** is:
1. Rivalry among competitors
2. Threat of new entrants
3. Threat of substitutes
4. Bargaining power of suppliers
5. Bargaining power of customers

## Q: What are the 4Ps? Give the question behind each.
@tags structure, academic, marketing
- **Product**: what are we offering? (features, design, branding)
- **Price**: how much do we charge? (pricing strategy, competitiveness; also financing terms)
- **Place**: where do we sell it? (channels, partnerships, locations)
- **Promotion**: how do we advertise it? (ads, PR, communication, loyalty)

They are also the four ways a company can compete. Product advantages get copied, so keep innovating.
