## Q: Brainstorm: what are the five categories of risk?
@tags risks, brainstorm
1. **Financial**
2. **Operational**
3. **Competition**
4. **External / market** (macro)
5. **Customers / reputation**

## Q: Brainstorm: financial risks.
@tags risks, brainstorm
- Lack of funding, cash-flow constraints
- Optimistic assumptions (revenue comes in lower)
- Unexpected cost increases, underestimated investment
- Currency and rate volatility
- Cannibalization
- Opportunity cost

## Q: Brainstorm: operational risks.
@tags risks, brainstorm
- More operational complexity, diluted management focus
- Supply chain disruption
- Poor quality control
- Capacity limits (production, warehouse, logistics)
- Delays, long negotiations with key stakeholders
- Talent and know-how gaps, learning curve
- Internal resources pulled from the core business

## Q: Brainstorm: competition, market and customer risks.
@tags risks, brainstorm
- **Competition**: price war or aggressive marketing in response, new entrants, consolidation, substitutes
- **Market / macro**: downturn, regulatory or legal change, uncertain demand, cultural mismatch in new markets, labor shortages, inflation, pandemic, new technology
- **Customers / reputation**: shifting preferences, product-market mismatch, brand damage or dilution, inconsistency with mission

## Q: Brainstorm: non-financial benefits of a move.
@tags benefits, brainstorm
- **Value proposition**: better customer experience, speed, quality, service
- **Capabilities**: new talent, shorter innovation cycles, scalable operations
- **Strategic**: brand, first-mover advantage, differentiation, risk diversification, access to new geographies, segments and products

## Q: For any market entry, opportunity, M&A or PE decision, what three things should you weigh?
@tags benefits, risks, brainstorm
1. **Benefits**: financial and strategic
2. **Risks**: brand dilution, complexity, talent, cannibalization, competitive response, cultural fit, integration
3. **Alternatives**: launch new products, other locations, improve the current market, acquire, JV, partner

## Q: Brainstorm: what goes into an implementation plan?
@tags implementation, brainstorm
- **Plan**: goals, stakeholders and constraints, schedule and budget, data collection
- **Execute**: awareness and marketing, logistics, pilots, partnerships, funding, quality control, building capacity
- **Long term**: track goals, sustainability, unintended consequences, hidden costs, opportunity costs

## Q: Brainstorm: how can a company make its suppliers more sustainable?
@tags operations, brainstorm
- **New suppliers**: switch suppliers, update selection criteria, move sourcing to countries with strict rules, integrate vertically
- **Existing suppliers**: educate and train them, run regular inspections, share sensors and software to track emissions, incentivize with SLAs and endorsements, bring in experts
