## Q: Name the single headline metric for each: airlines, hotels, retail, renewables, telecom/streaming.
@tags metric
| Industry | Headline metric |
|---|---|
| Airlines | RASK − CASK |
| Hotels | RevPAR = Occupancy × ADR |
| Retail | Sales / sq ft (and comps) |
| Renewables | LCOE (and capacity factor) |
| Telecom / streaming | LTV vs. CAC (ARPU, churn) |
| Banks | NIM |

## Q: Which industries are highly capital intensive, and which are lower?
@tags overview
- **High**: airlines, energy, telecom, automotive, hotels that own property
- **Lower**: asset-light hotel brands, software-driven media, fee-based financial services

## Q: Name five structural forces that cut across industries.
@tags trends
1. Consolidation through M&A
2. Digital disruption and disintermediation (OTAs, DTC, fintech, streaming)
3. Sustainability pressure
4. AI and personalization
5. Regulation as a cost and a barrier to entry

## Q: What four questions do you ask first in any industry case?
@tags case-angle
1. How does this company make money (streams and mix)?
2. What are the 2–3 biggest costs, and are they fixed or variable?
3. Which single metric best captures performance here?
4. What's changing in the industry right now that affects the answer?
