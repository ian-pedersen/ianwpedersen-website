## Q: A national gym chain wants to acquire a fast-growing app that sells on-demand workout classes, valued at $150M. How would you help them decide?
@mode recall
@tags m-and-a, structure
1. **The market (digital fitness)**
   - Size, growth rate, major brands, typical margins
2. **The target's standalone value**
   - Customer segments, product range, differentiation
   - Future revenue and costs; valuation multiples vs. the $150M price
3. **Synergies**
   - Revenue: cross-sell to gym members, bundles, new segments
   - Cost: shared marketing, overhead, procurement
   - Balance sheet and knowledge: tech talent, data, content
4. **Capabilities and risks**
   - Can we integrate and run a digital business? Are the brands compatible?
   - Market risks; integration, culture, operational, financial

## Q: M&A: what are the four types of synergy?
@tags m-and-a, synergies
1. **Cost**: consolidate direct costs (procurement, logistics, facilities) and indirect costs (duplicate overhead, sales, call centers)
2. **Revenue**: pricing power, cross-selling, geographic expansion, brand for the smaller company
3. **Balance sheet**: larger loans, longer supplier payment terms, consolidated capex
4. **Knowledge**: IP, talent and tech; best practices; easier hiring

## Q: M&A: what are the standard buckets for a strategic acquisition?
@tags m-and-a
1. **Target's market**: size, growth, competitors, margins
2. **Standalone value of the target**: customers, offering, future revenue and costs, valuation multiples
3. **Synergies**: revenue and cost (plus balance sheet and knowledge)
4. **Capabilities and risks**: can we execute and integrate? Fit with our portfolio, customers and footprint; market and client risks

Sources differ slightly: one folds the market into standalone value. Same content.

## Q: Why do acquisitions fail? List the main risks.
@tags m-and-a, risks
- Overpaying (optimistic synergy or growth assumptions)
- Integration difficulty: systems, reporting, processes
- Culture clash and talent loss
- Cannibalization of the acquirer's products
- Competitive response
- Brand dilution
- Distracted management

## Q: Before recommending an acquisition, what alternatives should you consider?
@tags m-and-a, brainstorm
Build it in-house, partner, joint venture, license, buy a different target, or invest in improving the current business instead.
