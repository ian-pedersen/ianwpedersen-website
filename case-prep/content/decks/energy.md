## Q: Energy: name the main player types.
@tags players
- **Oil & gas majors**: upstream (E&P), midstream (pipelines), downstream (refining, retail)
- **Renewables developers**: NextEra, Ørsted
- **Utilities**: regulated monopolies for generation, transmission, distribution
- **Equipment and services**: oilfield, wind, solar
- **Traders**: provide liquidity

## Q: What is the reserve replacement ratio (RRR), and what does below 100% signal?
@tags metric, formula, quiz
**RRR = reserves added ÷ reserves produced.** Below 100% means the company is producing faster than it replaces reserves, so future production will shrink unless it finds or buys more.

## Q: What is capacity factor? Give rough values for solar, wind and nuclear.
@tags metric, formula
**Capacity factor = actual output ÷ maximum possible output.**
Solar ~20–25%, wind ~35%, nuclear ~90%.

## Q: What is LCOE, and why is it the right comparison between a gas plant and a wind farm?
@tags metric, formula, quiz
**LCOE = lifetime cost ÷ lifetime MWh.** It puts capex-heavy, fuel-free assets (wind) and cheaper-to-build, fuel-burning assets (gas) on the same per-MWh basis.

## Q: Two solar farms have the same MW capacity. Why could their annual output differ?
@tags quiz
Different capacity factors: sun hours and latitude, panel tilt and tracking, weather, curtailment, degradation and downtime.

## Q: Energy: which oil & gas operating metrics matter?
@tags metric
- Production (BOE/day)
- Lifting / production cost per BOE
- Revenue per BOE (price realization)
- RRR
- ROIC = NOPAT ÷ invested capital

## Q: What are feed-in tariffs and renewable portfolio standards?
@tags trends
- **Feed-in tariff**: guarantees a fixed price to renewable generators.
- **RPS**: requires utilities to source X% of power from renewables.

## Q: What is a PPA?
@tags revenue
A **power purchase agreement**: a long-term contract to sell a plant's power at an agreed price. It de-risks renewables financing.

## Q: Energy: what are the main cost buckets?
@tags cost
- Capex: projects run into billions
- Opex: labor, fuel, maintenance, often in harsh environments
- Technology development
- Regulatory compliance: emissions, safety, taxes

## Q: Energy investment case: which inputs drive the NPV of a plant?
@tags case-angle
Commodity or power price, **capacity factor**, capex, opex, asset life and the **discount rate**. Run sensitivities on each.
