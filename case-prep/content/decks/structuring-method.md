## Q: What are the three steps to build a structure?
@tags structure, method
1. **Break the problem into first-level drivers** (3–5 of them)
2. **Order the drivers by priority**: lead with the one most likely to answer the question
3. **Add depth and insight**: second-level drivers and hypotheses tailored to this client

## Q: Two ways to find first-level drivers: top-down vs. bottom-up. What's the difference?
@tags structure, method
- **Top-down**: break the question into its logical components, often starting from a known framework. Needs the right logic.
- **Bottom-up**: brainstorm ideas that could answer the question, then group them into drivers. Needs the right insights.

Use whichever gets you there; check the result is MECE either way.

## Q: What is the AIM test for a structure?
@tags structure, method
- **A — Answer-focused**: aimed at the client's actual goal and question, with a prioritized path to an answer
- **I — Insightful**: tailored to this client's situation, not generic
- **M — MECE**: independent drivers that together cover everything important

## Q: AIM, "Answer-focused": what should you do before structuring?
@tags structure, method
- Restate the **goal and the real question** in one line, not the whole prompt
- Clarify only what changes your structure: objective, scope, anything you don't understand
- State your assumptions out loud
- Make the first driver the one most likely to answer the question

## Q: AIM, "Insightful": how do you check that a structure is tailored?
@tags structure, method
Ask: **would this structure work just as well for a different industry or client?** If yes, it's too generic. Add drivers and second-level questions that only make sense here (e.g. "are the two brands compatible?" in a fashion acquisition).

## Q: AIM, "MECE": what are the two checks?
@tags structure, method
- **Mutually exclusive**: no more than 4–5 first-level drivers at the same level, with little overlap
- **Collectively exhaustive**: every important idea has a home somewhere in the structure

## Q: How do you present your structure to the interviewer?
@tags structure, communication
1. **Signpost all first-level drivers up front** ("There are three areas I'd look at: first…, second…, third…")
2. Then go back and walk through the first driver in detail, with your hypothesis
3. Say why that driver comes first

Don't dive into detail on driver 1 before the interviewer has heard the whole map.

## Q: What dimensions does a case interviewer assess?
@tags method
- **Structuring**: goal, MECE drivers, prioritized approach
- **Math**: clear approach, correct and quick, implications stated
- **Judgement and insight**: prioritize new information, connect findings
- **Creativity**: many varied, sound ideas, organized
- **Synthesis**: a recommendation with key support and next steps
- **Case leadership**: drive the case, ask probing questions, adapt as you learn
