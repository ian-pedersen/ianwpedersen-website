## Q: Financial services: name the main player types.
@tags players
- Banks: deposits and loans
- Asset managers: mutual funds, ETFs
- Alternatives: PE, VC, hedge funds
- Brokerages
- Payments (PayPal, Stripe, Block)
- Card networks (Visa, Mastercard) vs. issuers; AmEx is both
- BNPL (Affirm, Klarna)

## Q: Banks: what is net interest income?
@tags revenue
The spread between what a bank earns on loans and investments and what it pays on deposits and borrowing. E.g. borrow from depositors at ~1%, lend at ~5%.

## Q: Define NIM (net interest margin).
@tags metric, formula
**NIM = (interest income − interest expense) ÷ interest-earning assets.**

## Q: Define ROE and the efficiency ratio.
@tags metric, formula
- **ROE** = net income ÷ shareholders' equity
- **Efficiency ratio** = non-interest expense ÷ revenue. Lower is better.

## Q: How does an asset manager make money, and what happens to revenue after a 20% market drop?
@tags revenue, quiz
**Revenue ≈ AUM × fee rate.** A 20% market drop cuts AUM roughly 20%, so revenue falls about 20% (more if clients also pull money), while costs stay largely fixed.

## Q: What is interchange, who pays it, and why does it matter for card rewards?
@tags revenue, quiz
A % of each card swipe, **paid by the merchant** to the issuing bank (set by the network). It funds card rewards, which is why premium rewards cards cost merchants more.

## Q: Rates fall sharply. What happens to a deposit-funded bank's NIM, and why?
@tags quiz, case-angle
It usually **compresses**. Loan and investment yields reprice down, but deposit rates are already near zero and can't fall much further, so the spread narrows.

## Q: Financial services: what are the main cost buckets?
@tags cost
- Compensation (usually #1)
- Regulatory compliance: AML, KYC, privacy
- Technology, especially legacy core systems
- Facilities and branches
- Marketing
- Credit losses / loan-loss provisions

## Q: Financial services: name the key trends.
@tags trends
- Fintech and disintermediation (Robinhood's $0 commissions, Apple's card and savings)
- Open banking and APIs (Plaid, Stripe, Adyen)
- Crypto and CBDCs, still uncertain
- Emerging-market challengers (Nubank)
- AI in underwriting and risk

## Q: Financial services: what are the typical case angles?
@tags case-angle
- Competitive response: a fintech is taking our young customers
- Profitability: NIM compression
- Due diligence: should a PE fund buy company Z?
- Growth: new card, wealth management, new region
