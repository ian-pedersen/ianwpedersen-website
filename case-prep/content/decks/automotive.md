## Q: Sketch the automotive value chain.
@tags overview
OEMs ↔ tier-1/2 suppliers ↔ dealers ↔ lenders ↔ aftermarket, all under safety and emissions regulators.

## Q: Autos: what is ATP, and how does it differ from MSRP?
@tags metric
- **ATP** (average transaction price): what buyers actually pay after incentives and markups.
- **MSRP**: the list price.
- The gap reflects **incentive spend per vehicle**, i.e. discounting pressure.

## Q: Autos: what are the main revenue streams?
@tags revenue
- New and used vehicle sales (often booked at wholesale to the dealer)
- Financing and leasing (OEM captives and banks)
- Parts and accessories (mostly suppliers and aftermarket)
- After-sales: service, repairs, warranty, insurance. High margin

## Q: Where does a car dealer make most of its profit?
@tags revenue, quiz
**After-sales**: service and parts, plus finance and insurance products. New-car margins are thin.

## Q: Autos: what are the main cost drivers?
@tags cost
- Raw materials: steel, aluminum, rubber, battery minerals
- Labor: wages, benefits, legacy pensions (e.g., UAW)
- R&D: EV platforms, software, ADAS
- Energy for plants
- Logistics: inbound parts, outbound vehicles

## Q: Autos: name the key trends.
@tags trends
- **Electrification**: still a minority of sales, long-run direction is clear
- **Autonomy**: early; Waymo is the leading US robotaxi example
- **Software and connectivity**: the fight to own the dashboard and its data
- **Tighter emissions rules**: push toward hybrids and EVs
- **Shared mobility**: may reduce ownership over time

## Q: ATP is rising but OEM margins are falling. What could explain it?
@tags quiz, case-angle
- Input costs (battery minerals, steel, labor contracts) rising faster than price
- Heavy R&D and capex on EVs and software
- Mix: low-margin EVs growing as a share of sales
- Warranty or recall costs
- Lower volume spreading fixed costs over fewer units

## Q: Why do OEMs care so much about owning in-car software?
@tags trends, quiz
- Recurring revenue: subscriptions, features, over-the-air upgrades
- Customer data and the direct relationship
- Brand experience and differentiation
- Avoid handing the dashboard to Apple or Google

## Q: Autos: which operational metrics matter?
@tags metric
- Incentive spend per vehicle
- Fuel economy (fleet averages for regulation)
- Dealer performance: sales per dealer, CSAT, **days of inventory**
- Warranty claims (quality proxy)
- Supply chain: lead times, on-time delivery, inventory levels

## Q: Autos: what are the typical case angles?
@tags case-angle
- Market sizing (e.g., US EV pickups)
- Investment: battery plant or autonomy bet. NPV, fit, build vs. partner
- Operations: supply-chain resilience (chip shortage), plant footprint
- Dealer vs. direct-to-consumer distribution
