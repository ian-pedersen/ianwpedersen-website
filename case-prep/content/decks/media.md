## Q: Media: list the monetization models.
@tags revenue
Ads, subscriptions, box office, in-app purchases, licensing and merchandise, live events.

## Q: Media: what are the main cost buckets?
@tags cost
- Content production (the big one)
- Marketing (tentpole films can spend $100M+)
- Distribution: theatrical, CDNs and hosting
- Talent and royalties (especially music)

## Q: Media: which metrics matter?
@tags metric
- Engagement: views, watch time, completion rate
- ARPU = revenue ÷ users
- Churn: % of subscribers lost per period
- Box office
- Streaming unit economics: CAC, LTV, content cost per hour viewed

## Q: Why is ARPU useful when comparing Spotify, Netflix and a free-to-play game?
@tags metric, quiz
It normalizes very different models (subscription, ads, in-app purchases) into revenue per user, so you can compare monetization directly.

## Q: A streamer's subscribers are growing but profit is falling. Where do you look first?
@tags quiz, case-angle
- **ARPU**: cheaper tiers, promos, mix shift to low-price markets
- **Content cost** growing faster than revenue
- **CAC and marketing** spent to buy that growth
- **Churn**: high churn means paying to replace subscribers

## Q: What makes IP valuable beyond its first release?
@tags quiz
It can be monetized again and again: sequels and spin-offs, licensing, merch, theme parks, games, and library value on streaming. Known franchises also lower marketing risk.

## Q: Media: what are the key trends?
@tags trends
- Streaming wars shifting from subscriber growth to profitability and bundling
- Consolidation (Disney–Fox, Warner Bros. Discovery)
- Franchise IP as the core asset
- AI personalization, ad tiers, password-sharing crackdowns

## Q: Media: what are the typical case angles?
@tags case-angle
- Streaming launch in a new country: local content, pricing, telco partners
- Revenue growth: ad tier, price increase, bundling, password sharing
- Cost: content ROI (cost per engaged hour), license vs. produce
