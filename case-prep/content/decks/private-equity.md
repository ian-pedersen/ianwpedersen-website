## Q: A PE fund is considering buying a regional chain of pediatric dental clinics. Should they? Structure it.
@mode recall
@tags private-equity, structure
1. **The market**
   - Size, growth, fragmentation, major players, typical profitability
   - Trends: insurance coverage, consolidation by other buyers
2. **The target**
   - Patient and payer segments, services, locations
   - Competitive advantages, management team
3. **Financial analysis**
   - Profitability: revenue drivers, cost structure
   - Investment: price vs. the fund's criteria (IRR, multiple, payback)
   - Value creation plan: add-on acquisitions, pricing, cost
   - Exit options and timing
4. **Risks**
   - Market (regulation, reimbursement) and target-specific (operational, financial, key people)

## Q: How does a PE case differ from a strategic M&A case?
@tags private-equity
A PE fund is a **financial buyer**: usually no operating synergies with an existing business. Focus on standalone profitability, the fund's return criteria (IRR, MOIC), the value creation plan, leverage, and the **exit** (sell to strategic, another fund, or IPO).

## Q: How does a PE fund create value in a portfolio company?
@tags private-equity
- Revenue growth: pricing, new markets, add-on acquisitions (roll-ups)
- Margin improvement: cost cuts, procurement, operations
- Multiple expansion: buy low, sell a bigger, better business at a higher multiple
- Leverage: debt paid down from cash flow
