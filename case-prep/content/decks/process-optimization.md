## Q: How do you structure "how do we optimize this process?"
@tags operations, structure
1. **Map the current process**: capacity, utilization, where the bottleneck is
2. **Look into each step**: eliminate it, reduce its cost, speed it up, or raise its quality
3. **Anticipate**: estimate the gains and what it takes to implement

## Q: A hospital's emergency department has average waits of five hours. How would you reduce them? Structure it.
@mode recall
@tags operations, structure
1. **Map the patient journey**: arrival → triage → wait → assessment → tests → treatment → admit or discharge; volume and capacity at each step; find the bottleneck
2. **Fix each step**: eliminate (send minor cases to urgent care), speed up (faster lab turnaround, fast-track lanes), add capacity at peak hours (staffing to arrival patterns), free beds downstream (faster discharges)
3. **Estimate gains** in hours saved per patient, plus cost and risks to quality of care
