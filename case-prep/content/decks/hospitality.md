## Q: Hotels: define occupancy and ADR.
@tags metric, formula
- **Occupancy** = rooms sold ÷ rooms available
- **ADR** (average daily rate) = room revenue ÷ rooms sold

## Q: Hotels: how do you calculate RevPAR? Give both forms.
@tags metric, formula
**RevPAR = room revenue ÷ rooms available = Occupancy × ADR.**

## Q: What are TRevPAR and GOPPAR?
@tags metric, formula
- **TRevPAR** = total revenue (all departments) ÷ rooms available
- **GOPPAR** = gross operating profit ÷ rooms available

## Q: Hotel A runs 90% occupancy at $150 ADR. Hotel B runs 70% at $200. Which has the higher RevPAR?
@tags quiz, formula
A: 0.9 × 150 = **$135**. B: 0.7 × 200 = **$140**. **B wins.**

## Q: Why might a hotel accept lower occupancy?
@tags quiz
A higher ADR can more than offset it (higher RevPAR), and fewer occupied rooms mean lower variable costs (housekeeping, amenities) and less wear, so profit can rise.

## Q: Why do big hotel brands prefer franchising and management contracts over owning properties?
@tags quiz, players
The **asset-light** model: fee income with little capital tied up, higher ROIC, faster growth, and the owner carries the real-estate risk.

## Q: Hotels: list the revenue streams.
@tags revenue
- Rooms (the core)
- Food & beverage
- Meetings and events (can be 50%+ at convention hotels)
- Ancillaries: parking, spa, resort fees
- Other: retail, gaming (dominant at casino resorts)

## Q: Hotels: what are the main cost buckets?
@tags cost
- Labor: usually the largest
- Real estate: owning or leasing
- F&B inputs
- OTA commissions, utilities, maintenance capex

## Q: Why are OTAs a "frenemy" for hotels, and how do hotels respond?
@tags trends, case-angle
OTAs (Expedia, Booking) bring reach but take high commissions and own the customer. Hotels push **direct booking** with loyalty pricing and perks.

## Q: Hotels: what are the typical case angles?
@tags case-angle
- Market entry: demand generators, competing supply, ADR/occupancy benchmarks, build cost
- Revenue growth: F&B, events, upsells
- Channel strategy vs. OTAs
- ADR vs. occupancy trade-off: optimize for RevPAR
