# Cross-industry cheat sheet

## Unit-economics metrics by industry
| Industry | Capacity measure | Price / revenue measure | "Fullness" measure | Combined metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Airlines | ASK | Yield / RASK | Load factor | RASK − CASK |
| Hotels | Rooms available | ADR | Occupancy | RevPAR = Occ × ADR |
| Retail | Sq ft | AOV | Conversion | Sales / sq ft |
| Renewables | Installed MW | $/MWh (PPA) | Capacity factor | LCOE |
| Telecom / streaming | Subscribers | ARPU | Churn (inverse) | LTV vs. CAC |

## Who's capital intensive?
**High:** airlines, energy, telecom, automotive, hotels (if they own properties). **Lower:** asset-light hotel brands, software-driven media, fee-based financial services.

## Common structural forces across industries
- **Consolidation through M&A**: airlines, CPG, media, financial services, telecom.
- **Digital disruption and disintermediation**: OTAs, DTC, fintech, streaming.
- **Sustainability pressure**: airlines, CPG, energy, autos, hospitality.
- **AI and personalization**: nearly every industry.
- **Regulation as a cost and a barrier to entry**: pharma, financial services, telecom, energy, airlines.

## First questions to ask in any industry case
1. How does this company make money (which revenue streams, and what's the mix)?
2. What are the 2–3 biggest costs, and are they fixed or variable?
3. Which single metric best captures performance here (RevPAR, RASK − CASK, NIM, comps, ARPU/churn)?
4. What's changing in the industry right now that affects the answer?
