# Pharmaceuticals

## Snapshot
- Discovers, develops and sells drugs. R&D-heavy (J&J spends ~16% of revenue), long timelines (trials take ~7 years), and heavily regulated.
- A delay costs serious money: estimates of the cost of a clinical trial delay run around $600K+ per day.
- **The core economics:** huge, risky up-front investment, then patent-protected high margins, then the **patent cliff**.

## Players
- **Discovery and development**: big pharma (Pfizer) and biotech (Genentech).
- **Manufacturers**: branded (Novartis) vs. **generic** (Teva).
- **Service providers**: CROs (IQVIA, formerly QuintilesIMS) run trials, and regulatory consultants (Parexel).
- **Distributors and retail**: wholesalers (McKesson), pharmacies (Walgreens).
- **PBMs**: Express Scripts, CVS Caremark. They negotiate prices and rebates for payers.

## Trends
- **Biotech revolution**: monoclonal antibodies, gene and cell therapies (e.g., Humira for autoimmune disease).
- **Patent cliffs**: Lipitor fell from a $12B+ peak after generics arrived. **Biosimilars** do the same thing for biologics.
- **More regulation**: safety, pricing, data privacy.
- **Future**:
  - **Rare diseases / orphan drugs** (e.g., Vertex in cystic fibrosis).
  - **Value-based care** (paying for outcomes).
  - Partnerships.
  - **Personalized medicine** (genomics).

## Money in
- **Prescription drugs**: ~70%+ of industry revenue. Oncology is the fastest-growing area.
- **Biologics**: the highest-priced products.
- **Vaccines**: a market of roughly ~$100B, boosted by COVID.
- **OTC**: Tylenol, Advil.

## Money out
- **R&D**: ~$2.6B per approved drug (Tufts estimate), including the cost of failures.
- **Manufacturing**: biologics are costlier to make than small molecules.
- **Sales and marketing**: sales reps and direct-to-consumer ads.
- **Regulatory compliance**: FDA.
- **Distribution**: the wholesaler, pharmacy and PBM chain, including rebates.

## Metrics
| Metric | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pipeline depth** | Number of candidates by phase. Future growth |
| **Phase transition / success rate** | % of candidates advancing (Phase I to approval is roughly ~10%) |
| **Trial timelines / time-to-market** | Speed, which means more years of patent-protected sales |
| **Sales per rep** | Commercial efficiency |
| **Patient outcomes** | Real-world value, which drives value-based contracts |
| *(Also)* **Peak sales, years to LOE (loss of exclusivity)** | Asset valuation |

## Case angles
- **M&A**: should we buy company X for its pipeline? Use risk-adjusted NPV (rNPV), check fit with our therapeutic areas, and size how much revenue we lose to the patent cliff.
- **Go/no-go**: fund Phase III? Use expected value = probability of success × peak sales economics − trial cost.
- **Market sizing**: patients with condition Y × diagnosis rate × treatment rate × price × compliance.
- **Launch strategy and pricing**, payer access.
