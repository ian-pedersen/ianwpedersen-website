# Financial Services

## Snapshot
- Complex and heavily regulated, and it underpins the whole economy.
- Examples of scale: BlackRock manages ~$8.6T in AUM, NASDAQ trades ~5B shares a day, ~60M Americans have 401(k)s, and the average American holds ~3 credit cards.

## Players
- **Banks**: take deposits and make loans (JPMorgan, BofA, Wells Fargo).
- **Asset managers**: mutual funds and ETFs (BlackRock, Fidelity, Vanguard).
- **Alternatives**: private equity (KKR), venture capital (Sequoia), hedge funds (D.E. Shaw). Mostly for institutions and wealthy individuals.
- **Brokerages**: Schwab, E*TRADE (now Morgan Stanley), TD Ameritrade (now Schwab).
- **Payments**: PayPal, Square/Block, Stripe.
- **Card networks and issuers**: Visa and Mastercard are networks; AmEx is both a network and an issuer.
- **BNPL**: Affirm, Klarna and other installment credit providers.

## Trends
- Deregulation and globalization.
- Consolidation.
- Heavy risk and compliance focus after financial crises.
- The rise of fintech.
- **Future**:
  - Open banking and APIs (Plaid, Stripe, Adyen). Visa's ~$5B bid for Plaid was blocked on antitrust grounds.
  - Crypto and CBDCs, which remain uncertain.
  - Emerging-market challengers (Nubank).
  - **Disintermediation**: Robinhood's $0 commissions, Apple's savings and card products.
  - AI in underwriting and risk.

## Money in
- **Net interest income**: borrow from depositors at ~1%, lend at ~5%, keep the spread.
- **Fees**:
  - Retail account fees (overdraft, maintenance).
  - Advisory and asset-management fees (% of AUM).
  - **Interchange**: a % of each card swipe, paid by the merchant.
- **Investment banking**: underwriting and M&A advice. Volatile, and concentrated among a few global banks.
- **Trading**: spreads, commissions and principal trading.

## Money out
- **Compensation**: usually the #1 cost (bonus culture).
- **Regulatory compliance**: AML, KYC, privacy, consumer protection.
- **Facilities**: offices, branches, data centers.
- **Marketing**: especially in cards and retail banking.
- **Technology**: legacy core systems are costly to run and to modernize.
- (Also **credit losses / loan-loss provisions**, which are critical in lending cases.)

## Metrics
| Metric | Formula / meaning |
|---|---|
| **ROE** | Net income ÷ shareholders' equity |
| **NIM** (net interest margin) | (Interest income − interest expense) ÷ interest-earning assets |
| **AUM** | Assets managed for clients. Revenue ≈ AUM × fee rate |
| **Transactions / payment volume** | Count and dollar value processed. The scale drivers for networks |
| *(Also)* **Efficiency ratio** | Non-interest expense ÷ revenue (lower is better) |
| *(Also)* **Charge-off rate** | Credit quality |

## Case angles
- **Competitive response**: a fintech is taking our young customers. What do we do?
- **Profitability**: NIM compression from rate moves or deposit competition.
- **Due diligence**: should a PE fund buy company Z?
- **Growth**: launch a new card, enter wealth management, expand into a new region.
