# Telecom

## Snapshot
- Moves information: voice, data, video.
- **Two sides:** **carriers** (services) and **equipment makers** (networks and devices).
- Arguably the most turbulent industry of the last 30 years: the internet, mobile and deregulation all hit it.
- Data points: Verizon spent ~$45B on 5G spectrum, AT&T's postpaid churn is below 1% a month, and US mobile ARPU is ~$35–50 a month. *(Note: the source lists "$2.25+ billion" iPhones sold. That's a unit count of ~2.3B iPhones, not dollars.)*

## Players
- **Carriers / ISPs**:
  - US: AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, Comcast (the largest US ISP).
  - Global: China Mobile, Deutsche Telekom, Vodafone.
- **Equipment**: Nokia, Ericsson, Huawei (networks), Qualcomm (chips and IP), plus device makers.

## Trends
- **Internet**: landline voice is shrinking fast, and broadband plus mobile data now matter most.
- **Mobile and smartphones** completely reoriented the industry.
- **Deregulation**: the AT&T breakup and new entrants brought more competition and lower prices.
- **Future**:
  - **5G**: speed, latency, capacity. Enables AVs, smart cities, AR/VR. Monetizing it beyond consumers is still a challenge.
  - **IoT** connectivity.
  - **Cloud**: hyperscalers (AWS, Azure) are major customers.

## Money in
- **Subscriptions**: mobile, broadband, TV plans. Recurring revenue.
- **Equipment**: devices (phones, routers), and for vendors, network gear.
- **Value-added services**: cloud storage, streaming bundles, advertising.
- **Enterprise**: networking, data centers, unified communications.
- **Roaming and international**.

## Money out
- **Network capex**: towers, fiber, data centers (e.g., AT&T ~$24B a year on fiber and 5G).
- **Spectrum licenses**: auctions can cost tens of billions.
- **Marketing / CAC**: ~$300 per consumer customer (Bain estimate). Plus device subsidies.
- **Labor**: field techs, customer care.
- **Regulatory compliance**: security, privacy, consumer protection.

## Metrics
| Metric | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **ARPU** | Revenue ÷ subscribers per month |
| **Churn** | % of subscribers leaving per month. Small differences compound hugely |
| **CAC** | Cost to win a subscriber |
| **LTV** | ≈ (ARPU × margin) ÷ churn. Compare it with CAC |
| **Network uptime** | Reliability |
| **Average speed** | Quality of service |
| *(Also)* **Net adds, capex intensity (capex ÷ revenue)** | Growth and investment burden |

## Case angles
- **Market entry**: a new country or a fixed-wireless broadband launch.
- **Investment**: a 5G or fiber build-out. Payback depends on take rate, ARPU uplift and capex per home passed.
- **Subscriber growth**: bundles, family plans, churn reduction.
- **Competitive response** to a price war.
