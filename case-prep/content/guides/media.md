# Entertainment & Media

## Snapshot
- Covers film/TV, music, gaming, publishing, sports and live events.
- Examples of scale: Netflix has ~230M+ subscribers, *Avengers: Endgame* grossed ~$2.8B, and Travis Scott's Fortnite concert drew 12.3M concurrent players.
- **Monetization models:** ads, subscriptions, box office, in-app purchases, licensing and merchandising, live events.

## Players
- **Film/TV**: Disney, NBCUniversal, Netflix. Streaming increasingly dominates distribution.
- **Music**: labels (UMG, Sony) and platforms (Spotify).
- **Gaming**: Tencent, Activision (now Microsoft), Xbox.
- **Publishing**: WSJ, Penguin Random House, The Economist.
- **Sports**: NBA, Premier League, F1. Media rights are their crown jewel.

## Trends
- **Digital disruption**: streaming, social, mobile.
- **Consolidation**: Disney–Fox, AT&T–Time Warner (since unwound into Warner Bros. Discovery).
- **Globalization** of audiences and content.
- **Fandom and franchise IP** as the core asset.
- **Diversity and inclusion** in content and hiring.
- **Future**:
  - Streaming wars, now shifting from subscriber growth to profitability and bundling.
  - AI personalization (Netflix recommendations).
  - AR/VR and virtual events.
  - Influencer marketing.

## Money in
- **Advertising**: TV, streaming ad tiers, social, branded content.
- **Subscriptions**: recurring access fees.
- **Box office**: ticket sales, a windowing strategy.
- **In-app purchases**: monetize free-to-play users.
- **Licensing and merchandise**: toys, theme parks, games built on IP.
- **Live events**: tickets, merchandise, sponsorships.

## Money out
- **Content production**: e.g., ~$15M per episode for *Game of Thrones*' final season.
- **Marketing**: tentpole films can spend $100M+.
- **Distribution**: theatrical prints and logistics, CDNs and hosting.
- (Also talent and royalties, which matter especially in music.)

## Metrics
| Metric | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Engagement** | Views, watch time, completion rate, shares |
| **Nielsen ratings** | Legacy TV audience measurement, fading as digital data improves |
| **ARPU** | Revenue ÷ users. Compares businesses across monetization models |
| **Churn** | % of subscribers or users lost per period |
| **Box office** | Still shapes greenlight decisions and talent value |
| *(Also)* **CAC, LTV, content cost per hour viewed** | Streaming unit economics |

## Case angles
- **Market entry**: a streaming service launching in a new country (local content, pricing, partnerships with telcos).
- **Revenue growth**: an ad tier, price increases, bundling, password-sharing crackdowns.
- **Cost optimization**: content spend ROI (cost per engaged hour), license vs. produce.
