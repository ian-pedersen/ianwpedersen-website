# Hospitality

## Snapshot
- Hotels, resorts, cruises, theme parks, casinos, timeshares. There are ~1.4B+ international tourist arrivals a year.
- **Three things to master:** service quality, the range of tiers (luxury to economy), and **real estate** (location is everything).
- Examples of scale: Airbnb has 4M+ hosts, Marriott has ~120K employees, and the Ritz-Carlton averages ~$1K a night.

## Players
- **Lodging**: Marriott, Hilton (many brands across price tiers), Airbnb.
- **Travel experiences**: cruise lines (Carnival, Royal Caribbean), tour operators.
- **Entertainment and recreation**: Disney parks, Universal.
- **Gaming**: MGM, Caesars, Wynn, Las Vegas Sands.
- **Vacation ownership**: Marriott Vacations, Wyndham, Hilton Grand Vacations.
- *Know the structure:* big hotel companies are often **asset-light**. They earn franchise and management fees while owners hold the real estate.

## Trends
- **OTAs** (Expedia, Booking): more reach, but high commissions. The classic "frenemy."
- **Airbnb**: pushed hotels to compete on consistency, amenities and loyalty.
- **Sustainability**: e.g., less frequent linen changes by default.
- **COVID shock** led to contactless operations.
- **Tech**: mobile check-in and keys.
- **Future**: personalization (e.g., Ritz-Carlton's guest-preference CRM), alternative accommodations, sustainability.

## Money in
- **Rooms**: the core line. Price varies by room type, location and season.
- **Food & beverage**: restaurants, bars, room service, catering.
- **Meetings and events**: weddings, conferences. Can be **50%+** of revenue at convention hotels.
- **Ancillaries**: parking, spa, resort fees.
- **Other**: retail, and gaming (dominant for casino resorts).

## Money out
- **Labor**: typically the largest cost (front desk, housekeeping, F&B).
- **Real estate**: owning or leasing the property.
- **F&B inputs**.
- (Also OTA commissions, utilities and maintenance capex.)

## Metrics
| Metric | Formula |
|---|---|
| **Occupancy** | Rooms sold ÷ rooms available |
| **ADR** (average daily rate) | Room revenue ÷ rooms sold |
| **RevPAR** | Room revenue ÷ rooms available = **Occupancy × ADR** |
| *(Also)* **TRevPAR** | Total revenue (all departments) ÷ rooms available |
| *(Also)* **GOPPAR** | Gross operating profit ÷ rooms available |
| **Guest and employee satisfaction** | Leading indicators of the guest experience |

## Case angles
- **Market entry**: should we open a hotel here? Look at demand generators, competing supply, ADR and occupancy benchmarks, and build cost.
- **Revenue growth**: what else can we monetize (F&B, events, upsells)?
- **Channel strategy**: how do we deal with OTAs? Push direct booking through loyalty pricing.
- **Raise ADR vs. occupancy**: they trade off, so optimize for RevPAR.
