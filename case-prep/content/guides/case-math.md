# Case math

The formulas behind the cards, grouped by when you'll need them in a case.

## Profitability

| Formula | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Profit** | Revenue − costs = (price × volume) − (fixed + variable × volume) |
| **Gross margin** | (Revenue − COGS) ÷ revenue |
| **Operating margin** | Operating income (EBIT) ÷ revenue |
| **Net margin** | Net income ÷ revenue |
| **EBITDA** | Operating income + depreciation + amortization |
| **Contribution margin** | Price − variable cost per unit (ratio: ÷ price) |
| **Operating leverage** | % change in operating profit ÷ % change in revenue |

## Break-even

- **Break-even units** = fixed costs ÷ (price − variable cost per unit)
- **Break-even revenue** = fixed costs ÷ contribution margin ratio
- Worked example: price $50, variable cost $30, fixed costs $400K → $20 contribution → 20,000 units.

## Investment and valuation

| Formula | Definition |
|---|---|
| **ROI** | (Gain − cost) ÷ cost |
| **Payback** | Investment ÷ annual cash flow (even flows) |
| **NPV** | Σ CFₜ ÷ (1 + r)ᵗ − initial investment |
| **Perpetuity** | CF ÷ r |
| **Growing perpetuity** | CF₁ ÷ (r − g) |
| **IRR** | The r where NPV = 0 |

> Quick check: $10M a year forever at a 10% discount rate is worth $100M.

## Growth

- **CAGR** = (end ÷ start)^(1 / years) − 1
- **Rule of 72**: years to double ≈ 72 ÷ growth %
- **Percentage points vs. percent**: 10% → 12% is +2 points, or +20%.

## Customers and pricing

- **Market share** = company sales ÷ market sales; relative share = our share ÷ leader's share
- **Elasticity** = % change in quantity ÷ % change in price
- **LTV** ≈ (revenue per period × margin) ÷ churn; lifetime ≈ 1 ÷ churn
- Rule of thumb: LTV ≥ 3× CAC

## Working capital

- **DIO** = inventory ÷ COGS × 365
- **DSO** = receivables ÷ revenue × 365
- **Inventory turnover** = COGS ÷ average inventory

## Market sizing

1. Clarify units, geography and year.
2. Choose **top-down** (population → segments → penetration → usage → price; usually preferred) or **bottom-up** (outlets or customers × units × price; best as a sanity check, since small errors multiply).
3. Segment where behavior differs and take a weighted average.
4. For durable goods, use the **replacement method**: annual sales = installed base ÷ lifetime + growth in the base.
5. Round aggressively, state assumptions, and sanity-check against something you know.

| Number to know | Value |
|---|---|
| World / US population | ~8B / ~330M |
| China / India | ~1.4B each |
| Germany / UK | ~83M / ~67M |
| US people per household | ~2.5 (≈130M households) |
| US life expectancy | ~80 years |
| US GDP per capita | ~$70K |
| US median income: individual / household | ~$50K / ~$75K |
| Work hours per person per year | ~2,000 |
